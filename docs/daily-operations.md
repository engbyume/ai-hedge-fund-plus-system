# Daily operations

## Before research

- Confirm the analysis date and market session.
- Check source freshness and provider health.
- Load the private preference profile and current holdings only from the authorized account source.
- Keep Fidelity-like and Cash App-like account state separate.

## During research

- Scan a broad approved universe before narrowing.
- Research individual catalysts first.
- Apply insider-selling, availability, theme-overlap, macro, and momentum-exhaustion gates.
- Preserve a countercase and invalidation for every serious candidate.
- Use forecast models as an ensemble or fallback, never as a sole decision rule.

## Before an action plan

- Reconcile current holdings and settled cash.
- Pair every sell with a buy or an explicitly approved proceeds hold.
- Block dependent buys until settlement is confirmed.
- Keep watchlist comparisons distinct from sell instructions.
- Render a no-send report and inspect both machine-readable and human-readable output.

## After the run

- Record delivery state separately from generation state.
- Record the operator's choice and any unfilled or unavailable order.
- Add a redacted observation to the evidence log when the date is comparable.
- Add a changelog entry when a rule, source, prompt, or integration changes.
