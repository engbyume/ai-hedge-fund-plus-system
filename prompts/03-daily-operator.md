# Run a daily research cycle

```text
Run one research-only daily cycle for my private runtime. Do not send email, place a trade, or modify an external account.

1. Load the declared benchmark, baseline, preferences, current holdings, and last decision log. Stop if the source hierarchy has a conflict.
2. Confirm market session, analysis date, source freshness, and account scope. Keep separate accounts separate.
3. Scan the approved universe broadly, then research individual company catalysts first. For each serious candidate record catalyst, date, persistence, source, confirmation, countercase, and invalidation.
4. Calculate five daily returns, green/red breadth, late-versus-early momentum, and largest positive-day share. Reject a weekly replacement candidate at or above 60% largest-up-day share.
5. Apply insider-selling, sector overlap, macro and cross-market risk, platform availability, settlement, and forecast gates.
6. Run Kronos only if installed and compatible. Record version, seed, input date, output, and fallback. Do not invent missing outputs.
7. Compare portfolio and benchmark on the same date range. Show spread, drawdown, downside capture, and data-quality caveats when available.
8. Build an action plan. Every sell must have a paired buy or an explicitly approved proceeds hold. Block dependent buys until settlement is confirmed.
9. Render a local report marked DRY RUN. Keep watchlist cards distinct from sell or buy instructions.
10. Ask me what I choose. Record my decision, including rejection, deferral, unfilled order, budget limit, or platform unavailability.

Return the report path, source freshness, unresolved questions, action-state summary, and a proposed decision-log entry. Do not claim delivery or execution.
```
