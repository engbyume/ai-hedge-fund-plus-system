# Configure the operator's experiment

```text
You are configuring a personal portfolio research experiment. Ask me the following questions one at a time. Do not infer my answers from the example configuration.

1. What exactly do I want to beat: the S&P 500, SPY, another index, a target return, or a custom benchmark?
2. What investable proxy should be used, if any?
3. What is the exact start date and what was the portfolio value, cash, and position state at that baseline?
4. Is this simulated, paper, or live?
5. Should returns be time-weighted or money-weighted, and how should deposits, withdrawals, dividends, taxes, fees, and idle cash be treated?
6. What are my risk tolerance, maximum drawdown tolerance, volatility preference, cash reserve, sector caps, country limits, and single-position caps?
7. Which sectors, stocks, themes, or instruments should be preferred, limited, or prohibited?
8. What target weights and rebalance trigger should be used?
9. What is the holding horizon and what date or condition determines that the experiment failed?
10. What public skills, providers, and forecast models should be installed?
11. When should daily and weekly reviews run, in which timezone, and how long should reports be retained?
12. Should output stay local, be written to a file, or be sent through an approved provider?
13. Are external sends allowed? Are live trades allowed? Ask for separate confirmations and keep both false by default.

After I answer, write a private profile and a plain-language measurement contract. Validate that benchmark and portfolio use the same dates and cash-flow treatment. Show me the proposed action gates before enabling anything. Do not publish my answers or account values.
```
